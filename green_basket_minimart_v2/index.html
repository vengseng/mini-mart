HELLO FROM GREEN BASKET

Everyday shopping, made friendlier.

Green Basket Mini Mart began with a simple idea: a neighborhood shop should make daily life a little easier. We bring together fresh fruit and vegetables, pantry staples, snacks, drinks, and home essentials in one welcoming place.

💚

A friendly welcome

We believe good service starts with kindness. Whether you are picking up one item or filling a basket, we want shopping to feel easy and pleasant.

🍊

Everyday essentials

From fresh produce to the little things a home needs, our goal is to help you find useful products without making shopping complicated.

🌱

Growing with our community

Every visit and every suggestion helps us improve. We hope Green Basket becomes a trusted stop for families, students, workers, and neighbors.
